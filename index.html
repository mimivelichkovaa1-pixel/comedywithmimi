About

Hi, I'm Mimi — a Bulgarian comedian, show host and performer based in Munich. I work the local stand-up circuit in three languages, depending on who's in the room and how brave I'm feeling that night.

Before comedy I spent years in consulting, event and destination management, and tour guiding — which mostly means I'm extremely good at herding groups of strangers somewhere on time and keeping them from wandering off. Turns out that's most of what hosting a show is.

Now I use those same skills to herd an audience through a set instead of through a museum, with a noticeably lower loss rate.

Fun & interesting info

Languages Bulgarian (native), English & Spanish stage sets, plus enough German to survive the audience and order a Brotzeit

Home turf Munich comedy clubs, including Lucky Punch Comedy Club
Best skill Making a room of strangers laugh in a language that isn't my first — sometimes not even theirs
